# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing anyone's private information (including data from their exports) are not
tolerated.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/atuuuuuuu/unpacked/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the
maintainer via their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or
contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously do so.
